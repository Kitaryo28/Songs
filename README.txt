SETUP:
1. Put your song in this folder and name it music.mp3.
2. Put 20 photos in photos/ named 01.jpg through 20.jpg.
3. Open script.js.
4. At the VERY TOP find:
   const lyricsText = `
   PASTE ALL YOUR LYRICS HERE
   `;
5. Replace ONLY the placeholder with your complete lyrics. Paste them once.
6. Keep the original line breaks. Do not add timestamps.
7. Upload all files to GitHub.

TIMINGS:
0-4, 5-9, 10-18, 19-23, 24-27, 28-32, 33-37,
38-41, 42-45, 46-51, 52-55, 56-60, 60-67.

TIMING_OFFSET is -0.30 seconds. Change it in script.js if testing shows
the lyrics need a tiny adjustment.
